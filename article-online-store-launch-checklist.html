Before You Launch An Online Store

A good online store needs more than product cards. Prepare your catalog, policies, payment flow, shipping details and customer support before development starts.

1. Prepare Product Photos And Categories

Use clear photos, consistent product names, prices, sizes, colors, stock status and short descriptions. Group products into categories that customers already understand.

2. Decide Your Checkout And Payment Flow

For India, many small stores start with WhatsApp ordering, UPI and manual confirmation. Larger stores can add payment gateway, cart, coupons, shipping rules and order emails.

3. Add Policies Before You Run Ads

Customers look for delivery time, return/refund policy, privacy policy, support number, address/service area and product authenticity. Missing policies reduce conversions.

4. Create SEO Pages For Categories

Category pages can rank for buyer searches. Add helpful text, FAQs, product filters and internal links instead of only uploading products with no content.

5. Keep WhatsApp Support Visible

Many buyers want quick clarification before paying. Add WhatsApp CTAs on product pages, checkout pages and post-order support sections.
