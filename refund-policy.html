Refund Policy

Last updated: 2 May 2026. This policy explains how refunds are handled for website projects and digital services.

Project Advance

Advance payments may be non-refundable once planning, design, content setup, development or third-party purchases have started.

Eligible Refunds

If a written money-back promise is included in your agreed project scope, eligibility will follow that specific promise, timeline and conditions.

Non-Refundable Items

Domain registration, hosting, paid plugins, premium themes, stock assets, payment gateway fees, third-party subscriptions and completed approved work are usually non-refundable.

Cancellation

If a client cancels a project, charges may apply for completed work, planning time, design drafts, development progress and third-party costs already incurred.

How To Request A Refund

Contact ktr.websolution@gmail.com or +91 9354170598 with your project details, payment date and reason for refund request. We will review the request against the agreed scope.
