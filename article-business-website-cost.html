How Much Should A Business Website Cost?

Website cost depends on pages, design quality, content, functionality, SEO setup, forms, hosting and support. The right budget should match your business goal, not just the number of pages.

1. Common Website Price Ranges In India

A simple static website can start from a few thousand rupees. Dynamic websites, e-commerce stores and custom features cost more because they need more planning, testing and setup.

2. What Changes The Cost?

Page count, custom design, product catalog, payment gateway, blog setup, SEO content, Google integrations, forms, speed optimization and maintenance all affect final pricing.

3. How To Avoid Overpaying

Start with clear goals: more calls, more WhatsApp leads, online sales or stronger trust. Ask for scope, timeline, included revisions and support terms before paying.

4. Do Not Ignore Maintenance

Hosting renewals, content updates, backups, security checks and small improvements matter after launch. A cheap website with no maintenance can become expensive later.

5. Get A Practical Quote

Share your business type, pages needed, preferred features and budget range. A good quote should explain what is included and what can be added later.
