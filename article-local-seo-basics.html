Local SEO Basics For Indian Businesses

Local SEO helps nearby customers find your business when they search on Google for services, shops, clinics, restaurants, coaching centers, repairs and professional help.

1. Complete Your Google Business Profile

Add your business name, category, phone number, website, service area, working hours, photos and services. Keep the details exactly consistent with your website and social profiles.

2. Use City And Service Keywords Naturally

Write pages around real searches such as website designer in Kanpur, salon website design, clinic website development or e-commerce website for clothing store. Avoid keyword stuffing; answer the buyer's question clearly.

3. Build Pages For Each Main Service

One homepage cannot rank for every service. Create focused service pages with benefits, process, pricing guidance, FAQs, testimonials, portfolio proof and a strong WhatsApp CTA.

4. Ask For Reviews After Delivery

Reviews improve trust and local visibility. Ask happy clients to mention the service and city naturally in their review, but never offer rewards for fake reviews.

5. Track Calls, Forms And WhatsApp Leads

Use Google Search Console, Google Analytics and a lead sheet to see which pages bring enquiries. Improve pages that get impressions but low clicks or visits but no leads.
